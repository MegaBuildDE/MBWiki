# Quests

Quests are your most reliable source of Mana. Open them with `/quest` or via `/sky` → **Quests**.

## Your quests

| Quest | Reward | Runs until |
| ----- | -----: | ---------- |
| **Easy** | 15–25 Mana | You complete it |
| **Medium** | 30–50 Mana | You complete it |
| **Hard** | 60–90 Mana | You complete it |
| **Weekly** | 250–400 Mana | Monday, 04:00 |
| **Bonus** | 120–180 Mana | Monday, 04:00 |

* **Daily quests** stay open until you complete them. For each completed quest you get a new one at **04:00**.
* **The weekly quest** runs from Monday 04:00 to Monday 04:00. If you don't finish it, it expires.
* **The bonus quest** unlocks if you complete the weekly quest within **3 days** – that is, by Thursday, 04:00.

## Skipping

If you don't like a quest, you can swap it for another task of the same difficulty.

| Quest | Cost |
| ----- | ---: |
| First skip per day (daily quests only) | free |
| Easy | 20 Mana |
| Medium | 35 Mana |
| Hard | 60 Mana |
| Weekly | 150 Mana |
| Bonus | 80 Mana |

## Pinning to the HUD

Click a quest in the quest menu to pin it to the top of your HUD. Then you can see its progress while you play.

## What kinds of tasks there are

Quests come from eleven themes: **Combat, Mining, Lumberjack, Farming, Fishing, Animals, Crafting, Exploration, Magic, Building** and **Trading**.

Typical tasks are defeating monsters, mining blocks, chopping trees, harvesting, fishing, breeding, taming, shearing, smelting, crafting, enchanting, trading, building, eating, walking a distance, visiting islands or biomes, and looting.

Harder quests sometimes have an **extra condition**, for example *with TNT*, *at night*, *in the rain* or *from at least 15 blocks away*. You get more Mana for those.

{% hint style="info" %}
**Quests match your island.** You only get tasks that are doable with your unlocked biomes – for example, no villager trading without a village biome.
{% endhint %}

## What doesn't count

* Blocks you **placed yourself** don't count for mining quests.
* **Stone from lava-water generators** doesn't count.
* For **building quests**, only what stays in place counts – if you break blocks again, the progress is deducted.
* For **walking**, at most 8 blocks per second count, and nothing after 30 seconds without moving.
* A **loot chest** only counts for the player who opens it first.

## Streak

The quest menu shows your **streak**: how many days in a row you've completed at least one quest, plus your best.
