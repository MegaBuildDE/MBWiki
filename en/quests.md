# Quests

Quests are your most reliable source of Mana. Open them **on your island** with `/quest` (also `/quests`, `/sky quests` or `/sky q`) or via `/sky` → **Quests**.

## Types of quests

| Type | What | More info |
| ---- | ---- | --------- |
| **Daily quests** | Three tasks – easy, medium, hard | below |
| **Weekly quest** | One big task per week | below |
| **Bonus quest** | For everyone who finishes the weekly quest quickly | below |
| **Biome and crystal quests** | Fixed tasks of your island that open new biomes in the skill tree | [Skill Tree & Biomes](skilltree.md#biome-quests) |
| **Special quest** | “Find the first resident” – once, right after you arrive | [Getting Started](start.md#the-special-quest-find-the-first-resident) |

## Daily, weekly and bonus quests

| Quest | Reward | Runs until |
| ----- | -----: | ---------- |
| **Easy** | 15–25 Mana | you complete it |
| **Medium** | 30–50 Mana | you complete it |
| **Hard** | 60–90 Mana | you complete it |
| **Weekly** | 250–400 Mana | Monday, 04:00 |
| **Bonus** | 120–180 Mana | Monday, 04:00 |

* **Daily quests don't expire.** They stay open – even over several days – until you complete them. For each completed quest, a new one arrives at the next daily reset at **04:00**.
* **The weekly quest** runs from Monday 04:00 to Monday 04:00. If you don't finish it, it expires.
* **The bonus quest** is yours right away if you complete the weekly quest within **3 days** – that is, by Thursday, 04:00.

Your quests count on every island you play on – your own and the ones where you're a member.

## Skipping

If you don't like a quest, swap it for another task of the same difficulty. You'll find the button for this in the quest menu below the quest. Before you pay, the menu asks you once more.

| Quest | Cost |
| ----- | ---: |
| First skip of the day (daily quests only) | **free** |
| Easy | 20 Mana |
| Medium | 35 Mana |
| Hard | 60 Mana |
| Weekly | 150 Mana |
| Bonus | 80 Mana |

The new quest is never the same task as the old one. Biome quests can't be skipped.

## Pinning to the HUD

Click a quest in the quest menu to pin it to the top of your **HUD** – then you can see its progress while you play. Without a pinned quest, the HUD shows the one you worked on last.

## What kinds of tasks there are

Quests come from eleven themes: **Combat, Mining, Lumberjack, Farming, Fishing, Animals, Crafting, Exploration, Magic, Building** and **Trading**.

Typical tasks are defeating monsters, mining blocks, chopping trees, harvesting, fishing, breeding, taming, shearing, smelting, crafting, enchanting, trading, building, eating, walking a distance, visiting new islands or biomes, and looting.

Harder quests sometimes have an **extra condition**, for example *with TNT*, *with bow and arrow from at least 15 blocks*, *at night*, *in the rain* or *with Fortune*. You get more Mana for those.

{% hint style="info" %}
**Quests match your island.** You only get tasks that are doable with your island's biomes – for example, no cacti without a desert.
{% endhint %}

## What doesn't count

* Anything outside **survival mode** and during the intro.
* Blocks you **placed yourself** – including sand or gravel that fell down afterwards.
* **Stone from lava-water generators.**
* Monsters from **spawners**, spawn eggs or commands.
* **AFK fishing** and fishing outside open water.
* For **building quests**, only what stays in place counts – if you break blocks again, the progress is deducted.
* For **walking**, at most 8 blocks per second count, and nothing after 30 seconds without moving.
* A **loot chest** only counts for the player who opens it first.

## Streak

The quest menu shows your **streak**: how many days in a row you've completed at least one quest, plus your record. It also shows whether your free skip for today is still available and when the next quests arrive.
