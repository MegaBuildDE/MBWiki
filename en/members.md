# Members & Rights

You can invite up to **25 members** to your island and decide for each of them what they're allowed to do.

## Inviting a member

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
#### Open the menu

**In the lobby:** `/sky` → **Members** → **Add a member**

**On the island:** `/sky friends` → **Add a member**
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
#### Choose a player

First you see your friends list. The search finds every player in the network, even offline ones – enter at least **3 characters** of the name.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
#### Confirm

Click **Invite**. The player gets a message and a mail and can enter your island right away.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

The owner and members with the **Manage friends** right can invite.

## The rights

| Right | What it allows |
| ----- | -------------- |
| **Join** | Enter the island |
| **Statistics** | View the island's statistics |
| **Settings** | Change settings and **buy biomes in the skill tree** |
| **Manage friends** | Invite more members |
| **Delete server** | Dangerous – not part of any role |

As the owner you always have all rights.

{% hint style="danger" %}
**Only invite players you trust.** Anyone who may enter your island can also **build, mine and open every chest** there – even with the Visitor role. Finer rights for building, chests and doors are planned.
{% endhint %}

## Roles

So you don't have to set every right individually, there are three roles:

| Role | Join | Statistics | Settings | Manage friends |
| ---- | :--: | :--------: | :------: | :------------: |
| **Visitor** | ✅ | – | – | – |
| **Trusted** | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | – |
| **Admin** | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |

If you change rights individually, the menu shows the role as **Custom**.

## Editing members

The member list shows your members' heads:

* **Left-click** opens their rights. **Change the role** cycles through Visitor → Trusted → Admin; below that you switch individual rights on and off.
* **Right-click** revokes the player's access.

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Whoever clicks, pays for the biome.** If a member with the Settings right buys a biome, the Mana is taken **from their account** – not from yours.
{% endhint %}

## What members get out of it

* Members help with the [biome quests](skilltree.md#biome-quests) and get **25 Mana** when one is completed – just like you.
* Their [quests](quests.md) count on your island too.
* Their playtime on your island is shown in the member list.

## Islands you're a member of

In the lobby, `/sky` → **Shared Servers** opens a list of all islands you're a member of. One click takes you there, even if the island isn't running.

To see which rights you have on an island, go to `/sky` → **Your rights** there.
