# Economy & trading

Players, trading and community should feel **alive** on MegaBuild.de and gain more depth through server-native systems than typical off-the-shelf solutions offer.

## What the economy is for

The economy is the link between what you produce and what you unlock:

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
#### You produce

Farms and generators supply resources.

{% content-ref url="farming.md" %}
[farming.md](farming.md)
{% endcontent-ref %}
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
#### You trade

Trading and the server-wide economy turn resources into value – with other players or through server-native systems.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
#### You expand

That value funds island upgrades and unlocks.

{% content-ref url="progression.md" %}
[progression.md](progression.md)
{% endcontent-ref %}
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

## Confirmed so far

* **Trading and a server-wide economy** as a dedicated system
* **Perks that are meaningfully integrated into the main game** – so no shortcuts that devalue progression
* Economy and community are deliberately designed together

## Ranks and shop

Ranks are available through the [MegaBuild.de shop](https://shop.megabuild.de/). What exactly they provide in Skyblock will be announced at launch.

{% content-ref url="ranks.md" %}
[ranks.md](ranks.md)
{% endcontent-ref %}

{% hint style="info" %}
**Worth knowing:** shop purchases support the server and are paid with real money. According to the announcement, the intent is to integrate perks so they complement the main game rather than replace it.
{% endhint %}

## Still open

{% hint style="warning" %}
The concrete economy systems have **not been announced publicly** yet. This includes:

* Currency and starting balance
* Player shops and trading spaces
* Auction house or market
* Server-wide prices and buy-back systems
* Commands for money and trading

Once the systems are final you will find the real values here. Announcements go out via [Discord](https://megabuild.de/dc/).
{% endhint %}
