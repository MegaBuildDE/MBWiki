# Mana & Ruby

MegaBuild.de has two currencies. Both work on all servers and are shown at the bottom right, above your hunger bar.

| | Mana | Ruby |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Used for** | Biomes in the skill tree, skipping quests | Trading between players |
| **Starting balance** | 1000 | 0 |
| **Sending** | Not possible | With `/pay` |

## Earning Mana

### Through actions

Many activities have a chance to give Mana:

| Action | Chance | Mana |
| ------ | -----: | ---: |
| Mining ore | 8 % | 1–2 |
| Diamond, emerald or gold ore | 35 % | 2–4 |
| Defeating monsters | 15 % | 1–2 |
| Fishing | 10 % | 1–3 |
| Trading with villagers | 12 % | 1–2 |
| Taming an animal | 60 % | 4–8 |
| Breeding animals | 10 % | 1–2 |
| Shearing sheep | 12 % | 1 |
| Harvesting | 4 % | 1 |
| Enchanting | 35 % | 2–5 |
| Every 5 levels gained | 50 % | 1–3 |
| Defending against a raid | 100 % | 20–40 |
| Earning an advancement | 100 % | 3–8 |
| Wearing out a tool (200+ durability) | 25 % | 2–4 |
| Taking items out of a furnace | 5 % | 1 |

{% hint style="info" %}
**Daily limit: 150 Mana from actions.** Quests and Mana Ore don't count towards it. The limit resets every day at **04:00**.
{% endhint %}

Advancements only give Mana the first time – no matter which island you're on.

### Through Mana Ore

Mana Ore looks like a glowing, magenta deepslate lapis lazuli ore. If you mine it with a **pickaxe**, you get **6–12 Mana** instead of lapis. **Fortune** increases that by 25 % per level.

Mana Ore has its own daily limit of **200 Mana**.

Quests give the biggest Mana rewards and have no daily limit.

### What doesn't give Mana

* Anything outside **Survival mode**
* Blocks you **placed yourself**
* Ore you mine **without a pickaxe**
* Monsters from **spawners**, spawn eggs or commands
* **AFK fishing** or fishing outside of open water

## Commands

| Command | What it does |
| ------- | ------------ |
| `/bal [player]` | Show balance |
| `/baltop [mana\|ruby] [page]` | The richest players, 10 per page |
| `/pay <player> <amount>` | Send Ruby |

### Sending Ruby

* There's **no fee** and no confirmation prompt – double-check the name first.
* You can also send to players who are currently offline.
* Amounts may use thousands separators: `1000`, `1.000` and `1,000` all work. Decimals don't.
