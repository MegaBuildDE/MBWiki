# Voting

Voting is the easiest way to support MegaBuild.de – it costs nothing and takes less than a minute.

## How to vote

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
#### Open the vote page

Go to [megabuild.de/vote](https://megabuild.de/vote/) – or type `/vote` in-game to get the link in chat.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
#### Enter your name

Enter your Minecraft name. Upper and lower case don't matter – but watch out for typos, otherwise the reward goes to someone else. Bedrock players enter their name the way it's shown on the server.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
#### Vote on the server lists

Confirm your vote on the linked server lists. Usually you can vote **once every 24 hours** per list.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

{% embed url="https://megabuild.de/vote/" %}

## What does it do?

### Your reward

| Per vote | |
| -------- | -: |
| Mana | **50** |
| Ruby | **5** |

* **Every vote** is rewarded – there is no daily limit.
* Vote Mana does **not** count toward the daily limit for actions.
* **Were you offline?** You still get the reward – with a message from *MegaBuild.de* in your [mailbox](messages.md).
* **Never been on the server?** Votes from the 7 days before your first join are credited when you join for the first time.

Apart from the 1,000 Ruby every account starts with, votes are the regular way to get more **Ruby** – the currency for trading between players.

### In-game

`/vote` shows you the link, your reward, how often you've already voted **today** and your votes this month and in total.

### For the server

Every vote pushes MegaBuild.de up the server lists and helps us reach new players.

## Top voters of the month

On the **1st of every month**, shortly after midnight, our [Discord](discord.md) bot posts the top voters of the previous month in the news channel. The reward goes to your account **right away**, along with a thank-you in your in-game [mailbox](messages.md).

| Place | Mana | Ruby |
| ----- | ---: | ---: |
| 🥇 1 | **750** | **150** |
| 🥈 2 | **500** | **100** |
| 🥉 3 | **300** | **50** |

* **A tie shares the place.** Two players with 12 votes each are both 🥇, the next one is 🥉 – and both get the prize for place 1. There are still never more than three top voters; if a tie reaches past that limit, whoever got there first is ahead.
* Only what you add during the month counts – having voted a lot earlier doesn't give you a head start.
* **Only names known on the server** make it into the ranking. If you made a typo on the vote page, that vote doesn't count toward the ranking.
* On Discord the bot reminds you to vote once a day and shows the current monthly standing of the top 3.

## Frequently asked questions

<details>

<summary>I voted but didn't get a reward</summary>

The most common causes:

1. **A typo in your name.** Then the reward went to a different name.
2. **You were offline.** Check your mailbox with `/mail`.

Nothing helped? Get in touch with [support](support.md).

</details>

<details>

<summary>Can I vote with multiple accounts?</summary>

No. Voting with multiple accounts or getting around the server lists' limits breaks the [server rules](https://megabuild.de/rules/).

</details>

<details>

<summary>Does my vote count while the server is in maintenance?</summary>

The vote itself is registered on the server lists. Rewards can only be handed out once the server is reachable again.

</details>
