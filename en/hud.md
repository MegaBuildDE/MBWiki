# HUD & Statistics

## Top: three boxes

On your island you see three boxes at the top of the screen.

| Box | Icon | What it shows |
| --- | ---- | ------------- |
| **1** | Globe | The island's unlocked biomes, e.g. `12/45` |
| **2** | Quest icon | Progress of a quest, e.g. `7/12` |
| **3** | changes every 5 seconds | Completed quests · playtime on this island · monsters defeated · players on this server |

**Which quest box 2 shows:**

1. the quest you **pinned** in the quest menu,
2. otherwise the quest you last made progress on,
3. otherwise the first open quest.

When a quest is completed, the box briefly shows a checkmark. If you have no open quest, it counts the blocks you've mined since joining the server.

In the **lobby**, the boxes show the time and how many players are online in the network instead.

## Bottom right: Mana & Ruby

Above your hunger bar you see your **Mana** (pink orb) and, above that, your **Ruby** (red gem).

## Statistics

{% tabs %}
{% tab title="In the lobby" %}
`/sky` → **Statistics** has three pages:

| Page | Contents |
| ---- | -------- |
| **Persönliches Dashboard** (Personal dashboard) | Activity, mining & building, progress, farming, combat, generation and your playtime on your islands |
| **Toplisten** (Leaderboards) | The top players in mining, building, harvesting, planting and monsters defeated |
| **Welt & Netzwerk** (World & network) | The most common biomes and island types across the whole network |
{% endtab %}

{% tab title="On the island" %}
| Command | Contents |
| ------- | -------- |
| `/sky` → **Statistics** | Mining, building, harvesting, combat, quests, skill tree, playtime, biomes and islands |
| `/sky stats` | Your stats in chat |
| `/sky global` | The whole server's stats in chat |

On other players' islands you need the **Statistics** right for this.
{% endtab %}
{% endtabs %}

## Playtime

```
/ot [player]
```

Shows your total playtime in the network and your first join – or another player's.

## Language

The menus follow the **language setting of your Minecraft client**: German for a German client, English otherwise. Some texts are only available in German.
