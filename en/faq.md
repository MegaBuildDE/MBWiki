# FAQ

## About the relaunch

<details>

<summary>What happened to MegaBuild.de?</summary>

MegaBuild.de is relaunching as **Custom Skyblock**: a fresh start with one main game, in which every player gets their own island server.

{% content-ref url="skyblock.md" %}
[skyblock.md](skyblock.md)
{% endcontent-ref %}

</details>

<details>

<summary>When does Skyblock launch?</summary>

No launch date has been announced yet. News comes first on our [Discord](https://megabuild.de/dc/) and on [status.megabuild.de](https://status.megabuild.de/).

## Getting started and technical

<details>

<summary>What's the server IP?</summary>

`megabuild.de` – for Bedrock, also port `19132`.

{% content-ref url="join.md" %}
[join.md](join.md)
{% endcontent-ref %}

</details>

<details>

<summary>Which Minecraft versions are supported?</summary>

The latest compatible Java version and Bedrock. Which Java version exactly is supported is posted on our [Discord](https://megabuild.de/dc/).

</details>

<details>

<summary>Do I have to accept the resource pack?</summary>

Yes. On Java Edition the resource pack is required – if you decline it, you'll be disconnected from the server. Bedrock players don't need to do anything.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Can I play on mobile or console?</summary>

Yes, via Bedrock Edition. On consoles, adding your own servers is restricted depending on the platform.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Can I use Java and Bedrock together?</summary>

Yes. With `/linkaccount` you link both accounts into one shared save.

{% content-ref url="connect-account.md" %}
[connect-account.md](connect-account.md)
{% endcontent-ref %}

</details>

## Skyblock

<details>

<summary>How do I get an island?</summary>

Type `/sky` in the lobby, select **Create Skyblock** and pick a **crystal** – it decides your starting biome. While Captain Mala tells you the story in the intro, your island is created in the background. Afterwards a Warpett takes you there.

{% content-ref url="start.md" %}
[start.md](start.md)
{% endcontent-ref %}

</details>

<details>

<summary>Can I change my starting biome later?</summary>

No. But you can unlock all other biomes with Mana in the skill tree.

</details>

<details>

<summary>How do I earn Mana?</summary>

Through quests, biome quests, votes, trading with AI residents, Mana ore and lots of normal actions like mining ore, taming animals or enchanting. Actions give up to 150 Mana a day, Mana ore up to 200 – quests have no limit.

{% content-ref url="economy.md" %}
[economy.md](economy.md)
{% endcontent-ref %}

</details>

<details>

<summary>Why am I not getting any Mana?</summary>

The most common reasons:

* You've reached your **daily limit** (it resets at 04:00).
* The block was **placed by you**.
* You mined ore **without a pickaxe**.
* You're not in **Survival mode**.
* The monster came from a **spawner**.

</details>

<details>

<summary>When are there new quests?</summary>

Daily quests renew at **04:00**, the weekly quest on Mondays at 04:00.

{% content-ref url="quests.md" %}
[quests.md](quests.md)
{% endcontent-ref %}

</details>

<details>

<summary>How many fellow players can I bring to my island?</summary>

Up to **25 members**. For each one you decide what they may do.

{% content-ref url="members.md" %}
[members.md](members.md)
{% endcontent-ref %}

</details>

<details>

<summary>Can I send Mana to other players?</summary>

No, Mana can't be transferred. Ruby, on the other hand, you can send with `/pay <player> <amount>` – every account starts with 1,000 Ruby.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Can I restart my island?</summary>

You can delete your island in the lobby under `/sky` → **Management** and then create a new one – even with a different crystal. Deleting is **permanent**. You keep your Mana, Ruby and achievements, but not the biomes you bought.

</details>

<details>

<summary>I bought a biome but can't find it</summary>

New biomes only appear in areas nobody has explored yet. A freshly bought biome is guaranteed to show up in the **next new area** – so fly or build in a direction you haven't been yet.

{% content-ref url="world.md" %}
[world.md](world.md)
{% endcontent-ref %}

</details>

<details>

<summary>Are the residents with "(KI)" real players?</summary>

No. Goblins and Shroomlings with "(KI)" in their name – German for "AI" – are AI residents. They chat with you, work and trade for Mana. With `/ki` you set what they may do around you.

{% content-ref url="residents.md" %}
[residents.md](residents.md)
{% endcontent-ref %}

</details>

<details>

<summary>How do I get more Warpetts?</summary>

From **Warpett eggs**, which you find as loot in chests, barrels, decorated pots and suspicious sand or gravel. An egg hatches in 10 minutes, on crying obsidian in 5.

{% content-ref url="warpetts.md" %}
[warpetts.md](warpetts.md)
{% endcontent-ref %}

</details>

<details>

<summary>Are there clans?</summary>

Not yet – the clan system is being planned. The [preview](clans.md) shows what's planned. Feedback on it is welcome on the [Discord](discord.md).

</details>

In the [shop](https://shop.megabuild.de/): **Gold** (€9.99), **Platin** (€19.99) and **Titan** (€49.99, currently on sale). The perks in Skyblock will be announced later.

{% content-ref url="ranks.md" %}

<details>

<summary>How can I support the server for free?</summary>

By voting on [megabuild.de/vote](https://megabuild.de/vote/) – or in-game with `/vote`. For every vote you get 20 Mana and 1 Ruby.

</details>
