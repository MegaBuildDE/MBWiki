# Community & events

Skyblock is designed as a **shared main game**: players, trading and community should feel alive rather than running side by side.

## Active events and community goals

Among the confirmed systems are **active events and community goals** – content the server works towards together, not just everyone alone on their own island.

{% hint style="info" %}
How events work, how often they run and what they reward will be announced on [Discord](https://megabuild.de/dc/) before launch.
{% endhint %}

## Discord

Discord is the central hub next to the server itself:

* Announcements about the Skyblock launch
* Supported Minecraft versions
* Events and campaigns
* Support and tickets
* Chatting with other players
* Feedback and suggestions
