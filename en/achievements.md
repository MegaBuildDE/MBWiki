# Achievements

Besides the normal Minecraft advancements, there's a tab of its own called **“MegaBuild Sky”** with **22 achievements** around the airship, Mana, Mobplates, the skill tree and quests.

* Every achievement counts **once across the whole network**. If you got it on one island, you have it on all the others too.
* The first time, you get **3–8 Mana** – only in survival mode, and it counts towards the daily limit for [actions](economy.md#from-actions).

## How to see them

| | Java | Bedrock |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **List** | Advancements menu (key **L**), *MegaBuild Sky* tab | Command `/geyser advancements` |
| **When you earn one** | Pop-up at the top right and a chat message | Pop-up at the top and a chat message |
| **Language** | Your client's language | Always German – the German titles are listed below |

### Clan

On top of that there are **8 achievements** for [clans](clans.md):

| Achievement | On Bedrock | What for |
| ----------- | ---------- | -------- |
| **Stronger Together** | *Gemeinsam stark* | Become a member of a clan |
| **Bronze** | *Bronze* | Your first bronze reward from the weekly task |
| **Silver** | *Silber* | Your first silver reward from the weekly task |
| **Gold** | *Gold* | Your first gold reward from the weekly task |
| **Hospitable** | *Gastfreundlich* | Welcome a clan member to your island |
| **Helping Hand** | *Helfende Hand* | Get rewarded for the first time for helping with a call for help |
| *hidden* | *hidden* | Two achievements around the clan island – they appear as soon as it's unlocked |

If you earn a clan achievement while you're offline, you get it, Mana included, the next time you enter an island.

## Good to know

* **Touchdown** goes to the island owner – only they experience the intro.
* Skill tree achievements go to whoever buys the biome. The owner gets them the next time they enter their island.
* Just like in Minecraft, you can earn an achievement before the one that comes before it.
