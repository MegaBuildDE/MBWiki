# Warpetts

Warpetts are small companions that **teleport** you within your island world. Which Warpetts are connected is decided by the **flower** you give them.

You have your first pair of Warpetts from the start: one on the **airship** and one on your **starting island**.

## How it works

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
#### Give it a flower

Hold a flower in your main hand and **right-click** a Warpett that has no flower. It eats the flower and from now on belongs to that flower type's network.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
#### Connect a second Warpett

Give a second Warpett **the same flower type**. Now both are connected.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
#### Travel

Click a Warpett:

* **Two Warpetts in the network:** You travel straight to the other one.
* **Three or more:** A menu opens with all destinations.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

Each of the **26 flower types** is its own network. If a Warpett is alone in its network, it tells you that a second one is still missing.

## Managing Warpetts

| What | How |
| ---- | --- |
| **Rename a destination** | In the destination menu, **right-click** the destination, then type the name in chat (max. 24 characters). Type `cancel` to cancel |
| **Switch network** | Give a Warpett a **different flower**. The old flower is gone |
| **Get the flower back** | Click the Warpett with **shears**. Costs 1 durability |

* There's a **1.5 second** cooldown between two trips.
* Warpetts only connect places **on the same island**.
* Until renamed, destinations are called *Airship*, *Starting island* or *Warpett* with a number.

{% hint style="info" %}
**Bedrock:** Instead of right-clicking, you use a Warpett by **sneaking** near it. You choose renaming in the destination menu with a toggle.
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="warning" %}
You can't get more Warpetts yourself yet.
{% endhint %}
