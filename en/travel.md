# Travel & Watching

You can visit other players' islands. If you have access there – as a [member](members.md), through your [clan](clans.md) or as a [friend](friends.md) –, you come as a **fellow player** with your rights. Otherwise you may look around the island as a **spectator** if its owner allows it: looking around yes, breaking, building and opening no.

## The travel menu

On an island you open it with `/sky` → **Travel** or with `/sky visit` without a name. It has two tabs:

| Tab | What's in it |
| --- | ------------ |
| **Open islands** | Islands where someone is playing right now and anyone may watch. The fullest are at the top |
| **Friends** | The islands of players who have added you as a friend – including ones that are asleep right now. The tab only appears when there's an island in it |

Each entry shows the island's name, its owner and how many players are there right now. Your own island, the island you're standing on and clan islands don't appear in either tab. The list is reloaded every time you open it or turn a page.

The last line of an entry tells you what a click does:

| Line | What happens |
| ---- | ------------ |
| **Click to travel there** | You have access there and come as a fellow player |
| **Click to watch** | You come as a spectator |
| **Asleep right now. Click to wake it and travel there** | You have access there: the click wakes the island and takes you there |
| **Asleep right now – you can watch once someone is playing on it** | Spectators don't wake islands. You can't click the entry |
| **Members only** | The owner doesn't let anyone watch, and you have no access there. You can't click the entry |

There's no travel menu in the lobby. There you travel with `/sky visit <name>`.

## Other ways

* `/sky visit <name>` takes you straight to a player's island – from the lobby too.
* In your [friends list](friends.md#your-friends-list) through a friend's card → **Visit their island**.
* In your [clan's](clans.md) member list with a left-click, or with `/clan visit <name>`.

If the island is asleep and you may only watch there, a line tells you that it's asleep right now. You can watch once someone is playing on it.

**Your inventory travels with you** – ender chest and levels stay on each island.

## Fellow player or spectator?

You come as a **fellow player** with the rights you have there:

* as a [member](members.md) whose **Join** switch is on,
* through the [clan access](members.md#clan-and-friends) or a [call for help](clans.md#calls-for-help) from your clan,
* as a friend of the owner through their [friend access](members.md#clan-and-friends).

If none of that applies, you come as a **spectator** – if the island allows it. That also goes for a member whose **Join** switch is off. What the owner can set for this is under [Watching](members.md#watching).

{% hint style="info" %}
**Spectators aren't visitors.** **Visitor** is a template for members and a level of clan and friend access – visitors may use doors and switches. Spectators have no rights at all on the island.
{% endhint %}

If you travel to a market through the shop search, you still come [as a customer](chestshop.md#as-a-customer-on-other-islands), not as a spectator.

## What spectators may do

When you arrive, a line in the chat tells you once that you're only looking around. You stay in Survival mode.

**Allowed:**

* Looking around and walking about, gliding with elytra and firing rockets while you do.
* Trading at the sign of a [ChestShop](chestshop.md) – in a closed [shop area](chestshop.md#open-and-closed) only if you have a shop on that island yourself.
* Talking to [residents](residents.md) and [Forestlings](forestlings.md), buying and using blessings, the Root Bridge too.
* Using the crafting table, stonecutter, loom, cartography table, smithing table, grindstone, enchanting table, anvil and ender chest with your own things. The anvil doesn't wear out.

**Not allowed:**

* Breaking, building, opening chests and other containers.
* Using doors, switches and pressure plates, harvesting, using animals and [Warpetts](warpetts.md), catching mobs with a [Mobplate](mobplates.md).
* Fighting – monsters and players too –, shooting and fishing.
* Picking up items, arrows and experience included. You only pick up what belongs to you, for example the rest of a shop purchase.
* Dropping items.
* Getting into boats and minecarts or riding animals.
* Flying with `/fly` – that needs the **Build** right.

If you try one of these, the display above the hotbar shows “You cannot do that here as a spectator.”

### Staying safe

* **No damage:** Only a fall into the void is deadly for spectators too.
* **Monsters don't attack you.** You don't trigger a raid, and sculk sensors don't hear you.
* **No pushing:** You don't push anyone and don't get pushed.
* **If you do die,** you keep your inventory and levels and lose a little Mana like anyone else – see [Dying](island.md#dying).
* **Full inventory:** If an item doesn't fit anymore, for example when you close a menu, it drops to the ground. Only you can pick it up – no other player, no hopper and no animal.

### When watching ends

* If the owner makes watching stricter so that you may no longer watch, you switch to the lobby right away.
* If you log out while watching, you land in the lobby the next time you log in – not back on the island.
* With `/hub` you can go back yourself at any time.

## Your own island

Whether and who may watch on your island is set on the island under `/sky` → **Members** → **Watching**: **Everyone**, **Friends only** or **Members only**. At the start it's set to **Everyone**.
