# Progression & upgrades

The guiding idea behind the relaunch: **every expansion, every upgrade and every step forward should feel rewarding and push you further.**

Instead of progression that runs out after a few hours, it is designed for long-term play.

## The three building blocks

* **Island upgrades** – Expansion tiers that increase space, capacity and possibilities.
* **Unlocks** – Features and content you earn, rather than have handed to you from the start.
* **Long-term goals** – Goals deliberately designed for many hours instead of being ticked off in one evening.

The three interlock: progress in one area opens up options in the others.

## Custom generators

A central element of progression are **custom generators** instead of standard routines. They determine which resources are available to you – and therefore how fast you can develop.

That makes the generator itself a progression goal: the further you get, the better its output.

## The loop

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
#### Produce

Generators and farms supply your resources.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
#### Convert

Trading and the server economy turn resources into value.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
#### Unlock

That value buys upgrades and new features.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
#### Scale

Those unlocks improve your production again – and the loop starts over at a higher level.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

{% hint style="success" %}
This interlocking is exactly what "progression that feels good" means: you should constantly feel like you are genuinely building something.
{% endhint %}
